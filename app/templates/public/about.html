About MediCore Clinical OS

MediCore is an enterprise-grade hospital and clinic administration platform engineered to provide medical practitioners, receptionists, administrators, and patients with an integrated clinical operating system.

Architectural Pillars
1. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Strict perimeter boundaries separating Administrator oversight, Doctor clinical privileges, Receptionist intake/billing workflows, and Patient self-service views.

2. High-Entropy Session Architecture

Production auth routes leverage CSPRNG 256-bit entropy tokens, server-side SQLite session management with HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookie delivery, and PBKDF2 password hashing.

3. Indian Healthcare Compatibility

Configured with realistic Indian (Kerala) regional profiles, Indian Rupee (₹) financial ledger, and outpatient consultation workflows aligned with clinical best practices.

4. Isolated Security Lab

Hosts two isolated, documented vulnerable blueprints specifically intended for authorized penetration-testing training. The lab is gated by localhost guardrails and configuration flags.
